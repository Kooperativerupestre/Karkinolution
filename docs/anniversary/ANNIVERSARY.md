# Karkinolution's First Anniversary (10/13/2026)

## Fun Facts About the Simulator

### What was the starting point of everything?

A video I watched late at night about a crab migration caused by heavy rain. It was around 3:00 AM, and the incident took place in an apartment.

### Did Karkinolution have any previous versions?

Yes! In the beginning, Karkinolution was called "Tempus serendi", a Latin name that would translate to *The Time to Sow*.

It was supposed to be a terminal-based game with multiple endings and philosophical questions. Later, I abandoned that idea, and the project evolved into a game where you played as a crab living on an island. Your goals were to cultivate the land, search for gems, and live peacefully.

Eventually, the idea evolved again: the game would be about wars between crabs.

### Did Karkinolution have a nickname?

Yes! It's called Karkino.

### Where does the name *Karkinolution* come from?

It's a combination of the Greek word καρκίνος (*karkínos*), meaning "crab", and the English word *evolution*.
